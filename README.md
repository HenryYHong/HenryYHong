## About Me

I'm a senior at UC Berkeley double majoring in Computer Science and Data Science with a Cognition Domain Emphasis.

I work with Berkeley AI Research on speech and machine learning research, and I've built production AI systems at Amazon spanning agents, retrieval, validation, and backend infrastructure.

I also teach on course staff for Berkeley data science courses, including Data 102 and Data 100, and previously served as Course Director for Data 198: Intro to Real-World Data Science.
