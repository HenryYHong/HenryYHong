## About Me

I am a senior at UC Berkeley double majoring in Computer Science and Data Science with a Cognition Domain Emphasis.

I currently work with Berkeley AI Research, exploring interpretable speech models. I also teach on course staff for Berkeley data science courses, including Data 102 and Data 100, and previously served as Course Director for Data 198: Intro to Real-World Data Science.

## Current Focus

- Interpretable machine learning and speech models
- Data-centric AI systems
- Full-stack projects with clean, useful interfaces
- Teaching, mentoring, and building learning experiences
